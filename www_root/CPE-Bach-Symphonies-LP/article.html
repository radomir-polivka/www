C. P. E. Bach: Symphonies – analogue recordings on LP

It is not so easy to get oriented in recordings of Philipp Emanuels' symphonies. Several verzeichnis systems exist and some LP covers do not say the numbers, just keys, which does not identiry the symphony uniquely. Hope this list can help you to find the right LP.

As ususal on this site, only stereo recordings from the analogue era are listed. Early mono and recent digital ones are omitted.
